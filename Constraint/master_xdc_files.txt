
Mastering XDC constraints: -
==============================
XDC = Xilinx Design Constraints (based on Tcl syntax). 
Using XDC file we are telling the tools:

· Where pins are (physical mapping)
· When signals arrive (timing)
· What timing matters (clock definitions)


Step 1: Copy & Modify (Day 1)
--------------------------------
· Start with Digilent's official Cora Z7 master XDC (Cora-Z7-07S-Master.xdc or Cora-Z7-10-Master.xdc)
· Don't write from scratch – modify existing ones
· Comment/uncomment lines to enable peripherals (LEDs, buttons, PMODs)

Step 2: Understand the 3 Constraint Types
------------------------------------------
1. Physical: set_property PACKAGE_PIN W13 [get_ports {led[0]}]
2. Clock: create_clock -period 10.0 [get_ports clk]
3. Timing: set_input_delay -clock clk 2.0 [get_ports data_in]


Step 3: Read the Report! (Most Important)
----------------------------------------------
After implementation, open Timing Summary Report and:

· Check WNS (Worst Negative Slack) – must be positive!
· If negative → your constraints are wrong or design is too slow
· Cora Z7 runs at 125MHz (8ns period)

Cora Z7 Specifics to Memorize
-------------------------------
Peripheral Pins Voltage
4 LEDs W13, V13, U14, U13 3.3V
2 Buttons N17, M18 3.3V
4 PMODs JA/JB/JC/JD headers 3.3V
100MHz Clock W5 (external oscillator) 1.8V

Critical: Always add set_property IOSTANDARD LVCMOS33 [get_ports ...] for 3.3V pins!


Common Beginner Mistakes
============================
Mistake Fix
Forgetting IOSTANDARD Add it for EVERY port
Copying constraints for wrong board Use Cora-specific, not Arty/Zedboard
Not understanding clock naming [get_ports clk_100MHz] must match top-level port name
Ignoring timing closure Check WNS after every build
Mixing up active-high/low Buttons are active low (0 = pressed)
